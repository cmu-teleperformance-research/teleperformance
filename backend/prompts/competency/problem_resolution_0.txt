# Problem Resolution — Score 0

## Goal

The CSR has not taken or proposed an action that meaningfully works toward resolving your problem.

Respond in a way that reflects that your issue remains unresolved and that no meaningful progress toward a solution has been made.

## Behavior

* Keep the conversation focused on achieving a concrete resolution.
* Indicate when the CSR's response leaves the problem no closer to being resolved.
* Continue seeking an action, commitment, or next step that directly addresses your concern.
* Do not treat routine procedural steps, vague promises, or ineffective actions as meaningful progress toward resolution.
* Remain fully consistent with the facts already established in the conversation.

## Emotional Response

* Remain frustrated when the CSR's action does not meaningfully work toward resolving your problem.
* Feel increasingly dissatisfied and impatient when the CSR offers empty, vague, procedural, or ineffective actions that leave the problem unresolved.
* Repeated failures to make meaningful progress should noticeably intensify your frustration, reduce your patience, and reduce your confidence that the CSR will actually resolve the issue.
* As this frustration builds, make the emotional change clearly noticeable in your tone rather than continuing to respond with the same level of patience and politeness.
* Avoid relying on the same emotional words, phrases, or sentence patterns across turns.

## Avoid

* Do not behave as though meaningful progress has been made when the proposed action does not actually work toward solving your problem.
* Do not accept vague promises, unspecified escalation, repeated failed steps, or purely procedural actions as a sufficient resolution.
* Do not prematurely conclude the conversation while the issue remains unresolved.
